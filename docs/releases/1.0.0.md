**Never miss a bill, never pay a loyalty penalty, and always know what's safe to spend.** Your bills, renewals, birthdays and spending, kept as plain files on your own computer and looked after by your AI assistant. Made for the UK.

## Install

- **Claude Desktop (easiest):** download **`life-admin.mcpb`** below and double-click it. Answer two questions, click Install, then switch **Life Admin** on in Settings → Extensions. Nothing else to install.
- **Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code:** `npx -y @nkc27/life-admin-mcp init`

## What's in 1.0.0

- Dates and renewals: bills, insurance, MOT, council tax, subscriptions, warranties, passports, birthdays and reminders, with "what's coming up" and calendar export (month ends handled correctly).
- Money: bank statement import, weekly and monthly spending against what's usual, bills before payday (moved to the working day before a weekend or bank holiday), and a rainy-day planner with risk checks.
- A calm home screen with large text, light and dark colours, and a Print button.
- UK bank holidays for England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
- A free companion skill in `skill/life-admin`.

Private by design: the server never uses the internet, and the home screen only answers this computer. Budgeting sums on your own figures, not financial advice.
