**Life Admin now works in any country.** Your bills, renewals, birthdays and spending, kept as plain files on your own computer and looked after by your AI assistant, in your own currency and with your own public holidays.

## Install

- **Claude Desktop (easiest):** download **`life-admin.mcpb`** below and double-click it. Choose your country, click Install, then switch **Life Admin** on in Settings → Extensions. Nothing else to install.
- **Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code:** `npx -y @nkc27/life-admin-mcp init`, then set `LIFE_ADMIN_COUNTRY` if you're outside the UK.

Already on 1.0.0? Nothing changes unless you set a country: it stays UK and pounds.

## What's new in 1.1.0

- Choose your country, and optionally your state or region and currency.
- Public holidays for about 200 countries and their regions (official gov.uk dates for the UK), used to move paydays and shown on the home screen.
- Money shown in your currency.
- Bank statements from other countries: semicolon-separated files, decimal commas, month-first dates and common European column names.
- Free-help suggestions that fit where you live.

Private by design: the server never uses the internet, and the home screen only answers this computer. Budgeting sums on your own figures, not financial advice.

Holiday data outside the UK: [date-holidays](https://github.com/commenthol/date-holidays) (CC BY-SA). UK bank holidays: gov.uk, Open Government Licence v3.0.
